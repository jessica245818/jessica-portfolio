# Plan to keep building

## Where the next case study goes

The next case will be added to the portfolio homepage directly below the working Content Opportunity Check. It will be the first full case-study section and will link to its public repository, README, and live demo evidence.

I will use the same three-beat shape every time:

1. **The problem:** name the person who needed help, the decision or task that was difficult, and why it mattered.
2. **What I did:** explain the workflow I built, the important decisions I made, where AI helped, and what I personally checked.
3. **What came of it:** show a verified result or working artifact, one honest limitation, and the next improvement. I will not invent impact numbers.

## Exact update steps

1. Gather the README, repository link, demo link, one clear screenshot, verified evaluation results, and limitations.
2. Ask the existing AI workspace to interview me one question at a time about the problem, my decisions, and the outcome.
3. Draft the case in my established voice: direct, warm, plain, and without buzzwords.
4. Add the three-beat case below the current live feature in `index.html`.
5. Check mobile layout, keyboard navigation, contrast, image clarity, and every link.
6. Confirm every claim against public evidence, then commit and publish through GitHub Pages.
7. Open the live page on a phone and update the fix log if anything breaks.
